1. DEFINITIONS & INTERPRETATION

1.1 The Consultant is a professional financial advisory and consultancy firm providing specialized advisory services, including but not limited to credit evaluation, document restructuring, and loan negotiation, assisting clients in obtaining approvals for personal financing, corporate financing, and asset management from licensed financial institutions operating in Malaysia ("Financing").

1.2 The Client is an individual or entity desirous of obtaining financial facilities and services from licensed financial institutions for personal or business growth purposes.

1.3 The Client hereby formally appoints TERAS LEGACY RESOURCE ADVISORY to act as its exclusive financial advisor, and the Consultant accepts such appointment subject to the mutual covenants and conditions hereinafter contained.

1.4 This Agreement shall remain valid, binding, and legally enforceable until all financial obligations and fee distributions are fully performed or terminated in accordance with the provisions herein.

2. TERMS OF AGREEMENT & CONSIDERATION

2.1 Consideration: In consideration of the professional services, documentation management, and financial advisory provided by the Consultant resulting in the successful issuance of a Letter of Offer or approval of the Financing by a licensed financial institution, the Parties agree to the fee structure outlined in Section 2(A).

A. CONSULTANT'S FEES & PAYMENT TERMS

1. Consultation Fees: The Client irrevocably agrees and undertakes to pay the Consultant a Professional Fee equivalent to 19% of the total approved Financing amount ("Consultant's Fees"), together with an additional 8% Service Tax (SST) calculated upon the Consultation Fee amount, payable upon successful approval and disbursement of the Financing.

2. Payment Schedule: Payment of the Consultant's Fees shall be settled strictly within one (1) business day (24 hours) upon the occurrence of either of the following events:

  • The Financing amount being credited into the Client's designated bank account; OR
  • Confirmation that the Financing has been disbursed to the relevant redemption banks/creditors as stated in the Letter of Offer.

3. Scope of Fee: The Consultation Fee covers professional evaluation, financial structuring, document processing, negotiations with bank underwriters, and administrative costs. No fee is charged to the credit institution directly.

4. No Upfront Fee Policy: The Consultant agrees that no upfront deposit or advance payment shall be demanded from the Client prior to the official financing approval.

B. CONFLICT OF INTEREST & LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

1. Scope of Role: The services provided by the Consultant are strictly advisory in facilitating the Client's submission to licensed financial institutions. Final credit assessment and interest rates remain under the sole discretion of the respective banks.

2. Institutional Independence: The Consultant has no direct influence over internal bank credit committees and shall not be liable for any institutional delay, interest adjustment, or partial rejection by banking institutions.

3. Client Representation: The Client shall not misrepresent the Consultant as an official branch or employee of the lending financial institution.

C. PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION & AUTHORIZATION

1. Data Consent: Pursuant to the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), the Client hereby explicitly consents and authorizes TERAS LEGACY RESOURCE ADVISORY to collect, store, verify, and process personal identification documents, bank statements, salary slips, CTOS/CCRIS credit reports, and tax documents exclusively for the Financing application.

2. Third-Party Submission: The Client grants full authority to the Consultant to submit all verified credentials to licensed banks or financial institutions on the Client's behalf.

D. COOPERATION & CLIENT UNDERTAKINGS

1. Best Efforts: The Client undertakes to provide true, authentic, and accurate financial records and respond promptly to bank verification calls or biometric attendance requests.

2. Validity Period: Should the Consultant be unable to secure a Financing approval within three (3) months from the execution date of this Agreement due to institutional factors, this Agreement shall expire without further financial liability on either Party.

3. Non-Circumvention: The Client covenants not to bypass, exclude, or deal directly with any financial institution or banker introduced by the Consultant for the purpose of avoiding fee obligations under this Agreement.

E. CANCELLATION & TERMINATION PENALTY

1. Unilateral Cancellation: The Client explicitly agrees that this appointment cannot be unilaterally canceled while the Financing application is active or processing in bank committee.

2. Cancellation Fine: If the Client cancels, withdraws, or breaches this Agreement while an application is ongoing or after a Letter of Offer has been approved, a mandatory liquidated damage fee of RM 5,000.00 (or full fee compensation equivalent to Section 2(A)) shall immediately become due and payable to the Consultant.

F. LEGAL RECOVERY & DEFAULT CLAUSES

1. Late Payment Surcharge: A default surcharge of 20% per annum shall automatically attach to any unpaid Consultation Fees if payment is delayed beyond five (5) business days from disbursement.

2. Credit Blacklisting & CTOS Reporting: In the event of willful non-payment, the Consultant reserves the legal right to report the outstanding debt to licensed credit reporting agencies (including CTOS, CCRIS, and legal recovery firms). A 20% Administrative Recovery Surcharge shall apply to reinstate credit status post-settlement.

3. ASSIGNMENT & JURISDICTION

3.1 Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed strictly in accordance with the laws of Malaysia. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall first be resolved through good-faith mediation failing which it shall be referred to the courts of Malaysia.

3.2 Entire Agreement: This document represents the entire understanding between TERAS LEGACY RESOURCE ADVISORY and the Client, superseding all prior oral or written promises.
