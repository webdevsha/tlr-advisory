Engagement and Services

Engagement. The Company hereby engages the Advisor to provide and perform the financial advisor services and the Advisor hereby accepts the engagement.

Standard of Services. All Services to be provided by the Advisor shall be performed with promptness and diligence in a workman-like manner and at a level of proficiency. To be expected of a Advisor with the background and experience that Advisor has represented it has, the company shall provide such access to its information, property and personnel as may be reasonably required in order to permit the Advisor to perform the Services.

Consultancy Fee and Payment

Consultancy Fee, In consideration of the Services to be rendered here under, the Company shall pay Advisor a Consultancy fee for Services provided.

Work Product and License

Defined, in this Agreement the term "Work Product" shall mean all work product generated by Advisor solely or jointly with others in the performance of the Services, including processes, know –how, ideas, discoveries, improvements and trade secrets.

Ownership, Advisor agrees to assign and does hereby assign to all rights of the Company, title and interest in to the Work Product. All Work Products shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company. Advisor will not have any rights and kind of whatsoever in terrible Work Product. Advisor agrees, at the request and cost of Company, to promptly sign, execute, make and do all Company's entire right, title, and interest in to any Work Product.

Advisor will not make any use of any of the Work Product in any manner without the Company's prior written consent. All Work Product shall be promptly communicated to Company.

License. In the event that Advisor integrates any work that was previously created by the Advisor into any Work Product, the Advisor shall grant to, and Company is here by granted, a worldwide, royalty fee, perpetual, irrevocable license to exploit the incorporated items, including, but not limited to, any and all copyright, patents, design, trade secrets, trademarks or other intellectual property rights, in connection with the Work Product in any manner that Company deems appropriate. Confidential Information

Defined, In this Agreement the term "Confidential Information" shall mean the Work Product and any all information relating to Company's business, including, but not limited to, research, developments, product, services, diagrams, formulae, processes, techniques, technology, firmware, software, know-how, design, ideas, discoveries, inventions, improvements, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, customer, suppliers, markets, marketing, finances disclosed by Company either directly or indirectly in writing, orally or visually, to Advisor. Confidential Information does not include information which:

  • is in or comes into the public domain without breach of this Agreement by Advisor.
  • was in the possession of the Advisor prior to receipt from Company and was not acquired by the Advisor from the Company under an obligation of confidentiality or non-use to the Company, or
  • is independently developed by Advisor without use of any Confidential Information of the Company.

Obligation of Non Disclosure and Non-Use. Unless otherwise agreed to in advance and in Writing by the Company, Advisor will not, except as required by law or court order, use the Confidential Information to any third party. Advisor may disclose the Confidential Information only to those of its employees who need to know such information. In addition, prior to any disclosure of such Confidential Information to any such employee, such employee shall be made aware of the confidential nature of the Confidential Information and shall execute, or shall already be bounded by a non-disclosure agreement containing the terms and conditions consistent with the terms and conditions of this Agreement. In any event, Advisor shall be responsible for any breach of the terms and conditions of this Agreement by any of its employees. Advisor shall use the same degree of care to avoid disclosure of the Confidential Information as it employs with respect to its own Confidential Information of like importance, but not less than a reasonable degree of care.

Return of Confidential Information. Upon the termination or expiration of this Agreement for any reason, or upon Company's earlier request, Advisor will deliver to Company all of Company's property or Confidential Information in tangible form that Advisor may have in its possession or control. The Advisor may retain one copy of the Confidential Information in its legal files.

Independent Contractor

The Advisor agrees that all Services will be rendered by it as an independent contractor and that this Agreement does not create an employer - employee relationship between the Advisor and the Company. The Advisor shall have no right to receive any employee benefits provided by the Company to its employees. Advisor agrees to pay all the taxes due in respect of the Consultancy Fee and to indemnify the Company in respect of any obligation that may be imposed on the Company to pay any such taxes or resulting from Advisor's being determined not to be an independent contractor. This Agreement does not authorize the Advisor to act for the Company as its agent or to make commitments on behalf of the Company.

Non-Publicity

Each of Company and Advisor agrees not to disclose the existence or contents of this Agreement to any third party without the prior written consent of the other parties except: (i) to its advisors, attorneys or auditors who haves to know such information, (ii) as required by law or court order, (iii) as required in connection with the reorganization of the Party, or its merger into any other corporations, or the sale by Party of all or substantially all of its properties or assets, or (iv) as may be required in connection with the enforcement of this Agreement.

Assignment

The Services to be performed by Advisor hereunder are personal in nature, and Company has engaged Advisor as a result of Advisor's expertise relating to such Services. Advisor, therefore, agrees that it will not assign, sell, transfer, delegate or otherwise dispose this Agreement or any right, duty or obligation under this Agreement without the Company's prior written consent. Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent the assignment by the Company of this Agreement or any right, duty or obligation hereunder to any third party.

This Agreement may not be modified other than by a written instrument executed by duly authorized representatives of the Parties. No waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall constitute a waiver of any other provision(s) or of the same provision on another occasion.
